Getting started

Use it in three steps

01 · Open a lesson

Select a lecture card and choose Open in Colab. It launches a copy of the notebook in your browser; you do not need to install Python.

02 · Download the lecture data

For notebooks requiring datasets, get the corresponding ZIP on the lecture page and upload it when Colab prompts you.

03 · Run and save your work

Choose Runtime → Run all, or execute cells one by one. Save a personal copy to your own Google Drive if you want to keep your changes.

What belongs on this site?
